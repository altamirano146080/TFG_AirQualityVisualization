\chapter*{Abstract}
Air pollution is a major environmental and health challenge, causing millions of premature deaths and severe impacts on ecosystems and climate. Reliable forecasts are essential for public policies, awareness, and daily decision-making. The Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) provides high-quality forecasts of atmospheric composition worldwide, but current platforms show a trade-off: scientific tools are too complex for non-experts, while public-oriented applications oversimplify the information.

This work presents an online visualization platform that bridges this gap by offering an accessible yet analytically robust interface to explore CAMS forecasts. The system runs entirely in the browser and is built with open-source technologies such as Leaflet.js for interactive mapping and Highcharts for time-series visualization. Key features include an hourly forecast time slider, pollutant value extraction at any clicked location, interactive historical charts, customizable color scales, and animation of forecast evolution.

The results show that the platform successfully combines usability and analytical capability, enabling users to interpret complex atmospheric forecasts without specialized software. By standardizing pollutant units and integrating adaptive legends, the system ensures consistency and clarity. Beyond its academic contribution, the tool supports education, public awareness, and environmental monitoring. Future work will focus on integrating observational datasets for validation and adding advanced statistical analyses.
