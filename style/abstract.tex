\chapter*{Abstract}
Air pollution is one of today’s most critical environmental and public health challenges, causing millions of premature deaths and serious impacts on ecosystems and climate. Reliable forecasts are essential for decision-making and awareness. The Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) provides global forecasts, but current platforms are either too technical for non-experts or oversimplify the data.

This work presents a lightweight web-based visualization platform that bridges this gap. Built entirely with open-source tools such as Leaflet.js and Highcharts, it allows users to interactively explore CAMS forecasts through hourly time sliders, pollutant value queries, historical charts, adaptive legends, and forecast animations.
